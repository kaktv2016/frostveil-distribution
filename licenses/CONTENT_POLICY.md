# Content and permission policy

L4-B distributes only original generated harmless notice/banner text in disposable sandboxes. No Skyrim, DLC, SKSE, SkyMP DLLs, Nexus or other third-party assets are included. Native-looking synthetic verification bytes never become Release Assets.

Owned files require original provenance. Permitted third-party files require recorded evidence, redistribution scope, reviewer and a reviewed permission fingerprint pinned in trusted publisher configuration. Credit is insufficient. External-source/runtime/unverified components remain manual. Signing alone cannot authorize rights. Third-party licenses retain their original terms; this repository does not invent a license for them.

Project source is published by its owner for audit. No general redistribution license is granted by this policy. Node/npm dependencies remain separately licensed and are installed from pinned lockfiles; their code is not vendored here.
